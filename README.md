# Sales Dashboard
Purpose:
The dashboard allows users to track key sales metrics, analyze profitability trends, and explore sales data across different dimensions, such as region, channel, product category, and manufacturer.

Key Metrics:
Total Sales: The overall revenue generated from all sales transactions.
Total Profit: The total profit made from sales after deducting costs.
Order Quantity: The total count of orders
Average Order Value: measures the average amount of money customers spend per order (AOV = Total Revenue / Total Number of Orders) 

Visuals:
1. Sales & Profit Over Time (Line Chart)
This line chart shows how sales and profit have evolved over time. It helps track trends and seasonal variations in revenue and profitability. The chart allows users to assess the overall performance of the business over a given period.

2. Sales by Region (Pie Chart)
This pie chart breaks down total sales by region, visually highlighting the contribution of each region to the overall sales volume. It provides insights into geographic performance, helping to identify strong and weak markets.

3. Sales by Channel (Pie Chart)
This pie chart shows the distribution of sales across different sales channels (e.g., online, in-store, etc.). It helps visualize which channels are driving the most sales, providing insight into where the business is performing best.

4. Sales & Profit by Product Category (Bar Chart)
A bar chart comparing sales and profit by product category. This visual helps identify the most profitable categories and allows businesses to analyze which product types are driving both revenue and profit.

5. Sales & Profit by Manufacturer (Stacked Column Chart)
A stacked column chart comparing sales and profit by manufacturer. This chart allows you to see both total sales and profits for each manufacturer in one view, helping to understand how each manufacturer contributes to the overall performance.

6. Sales by City (Map)
A map visualization shows sales by city, with a color gradient indicating sales performance. Additionally, profit is represented as a bubble, where the size of the bubble corresponds to the total profit in each city. This helps identify cities with the highest sales, while also highlighting those with the highest profitability.



# Product performance dashbaord

Purpose:
The Product Dashboard is designed to analyse product sales, profitability, and performance by category. The dashboard helps identify top-performing products, understand profit margins, and assess the overall sales landscape.

Key Metrics:
Sales: The total revenue generated from each product.

Profit: The total profit from each product after deducting costs.

Average Unit Cost: The average cost price of products in each category.

Average Profit Margin: The percentage of profit relative to the sales price of each product.



Visuals:
1. Product Category by Sales (Bar Chart)
This bar chart shows the total sales generated by each product category. It helps quickly identify the top-performing categories in terms of revenue.

2. Product Category (Dual Axis: Unit Price, Cost)
This dual-axis chart compares the unit price and the unit cost of products within each category, helping to analyze pricing and cost efficiency.

3. Product by Sales (Descending and Ascending for Top and Bottom Performers)
A bar chart displaying products sorted by sales, both in ascending and descending order, allowing for a quick view of the best and worst-selling products.

4. Pivot Table (Category, Sub-Category by Sales, Profit, Avg Profit Margin)
This pivot table presents sales, profit, and average profit margin broken down by category and sub-category. It provides a more granular look at product performance.

5. Product Category by Sales Over Year-Month (Area Chart)
This area chart visualizes the sales trends for each product category over time (broken down by month and year), allowing you to see how sales have evolved.

6. Sales vs. Profit by Order Quantity (Bubble Chart)
A bubble chart showing the relationship between sales, profit, and order quantity. The size of each bubble corresponds to the order quantity, while the x and y axes show sales and profit.

7. Profit by Product Category, Sub-Category (Tree Map)
This tree map visualizes profit by category and sub-category, with larger blocks representing higher profits. It's useful for identifying profitable areas within your product portfolio.


The dashboards allows for interactive filtering by product category, Order date, Country and region, helping users drill down into specific product performance details.

Visuals are designed for easy interpretation of sales trends, profitability, and cost management, providing valuable insights to improve decision-making.
