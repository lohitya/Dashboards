# Superstore Dashboard

**Business Problem**

The company is seeing inconsistent profitability across Canada and US states and product categories. While sales volume is strong in certain areas, leadership lacks visibility into which segments and product lines are truly driving performance — and which may be hurting margins. A better understanding of geographic and segment-based performance is needed to optimize product focus and regional strategy.


**Key Insights**

Sales Concentration: Most orders fall in the $0–$999 sales bin, with smaller sales driving the highest volume, order count, and profit.

Top State: California leads in both total sales and quantity sold, making it the most profitable consumer market.

Segment Performance: The Consumer segment delivers the highest sales and profit, outperforming Corporate and Home Office.

Category Analysis: Office Supplies is the most frequently sold product category, though it may not always bring the highest profit margin.

**Recommendations**

Double down on the Consumer segment, particularly in high-performing states like California.

Refine the product strategy by promoting high-volume, high-margin items within Office Supplies and related sub-categories.

Explore opportunities in lower-performing states by replicating marketing and bundling strategies used in top-performing regions.
