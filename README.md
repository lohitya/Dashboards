# Sales, Product, Consumer, and Order Fulfillment/Store Performance Dashboard

Overview

This repository contains Power BI dashboards designed to provide insights into business performance using a transaction dataset. The dashboards focus on key business areas, enabling effective decision-making through interactive visuals and KPIs.

1. Sales Dashboard

KPIs:

    Total Sales,  Profit, Total Orders, Quantity Sold, Total Customers, Average Delivery Time

Visuals:

    Sales & Profit by Continent (Line Chart)
    
    Sales Growth % by Year (Line Chart)
    
    Profit by Year (Line Chart)
    
    Sales & Profit by Category (Bar Chart)
    
    Country-wise Sales, Profit, Quantity (Matrix)

Slicers:

    Date
    
    Product Category
    
    Store Type (Online, Physical)
    
    Continent

Analysis:

This dashboard tracks sales performance over time, highlights trends in profitability, and provides a geographical breakdown of sales. It leverages calculated fields and a custom date table for accurate time-based analysis.

# 2. Product Dashboard

KPIs:

    Average Cost (Measure), Average Price (Measure), Average Delivery Days (Measure), Total Sales, Profit, Quantity Sold

Visuals:

    Sales, Profit & Quantity by Category, Sub-Category (Matrix)
    
    Total Profit (Grand Total) (Matrix)
    
    Sales by Product Brand (Tree Map)
    
    Product Profitability (Scatter Plot with Avg Cost/Price Reference Lines)
    
    Top 5/Bottom 5 Products by Sales with its Profit, Quantity (Column Chart)
    
    Slicers:
    
    Top 5/Bottom 5 - created using Calculated Column as slicer 

Analysis:

The Product Dashboard offers detailed insights into product performance, pricing strategy, and profitability. The scatter plot efficiently visualizes cost and pricing efficiency, while the calculated column slicer pinpoints top and bottom performers.
